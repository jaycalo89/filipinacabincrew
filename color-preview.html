Colour preview — current vs logo-anchored

A standalone review page. It links no site stylesheet and changes nothing: css/style.css and every existing page are untouched. Both columns below render identical markup — the only difference is the token block applied to each.

The shift: every blue in the logo sits at hue 207–211°. The current navy ramp sits at 227–229° and the tinted neutrals at 215–224°. That ~21° lean toward violet is what you were seeing. The proposed ramp holds hue 207.5° throughout, and the neutrals are retinted to 207°.

1. Blue ramp

Contrast is measured against white, which is the background everywhere these tokens are used. Lightness stays close to the current ramp so the visual hierarchy survives; saturation rises at the dark end because the logo navy is fully saturated.

2. Blue-tinted neutrals

Saturation and lightness are held; only hue moves. These are not brand blues, but they carry the same violet lean — leaving them behind is what would make a corrected navy look mismatched against its own borders and body text. The hue shown on each swatch is the hue the rounded 8-bit value actually lands on: at very high lightness and low saturation a hex can only approximate 207°, so the palest neutrals read a few degrees either side of it.

One deliberate exception. A straight hue-only retint of --ink-muted gives #6B7F90 at 4.15:1, which fails WCAG AA for normal text. That token sets card meta, listing timestamps and the per-card disclaimer — all normal-size body copy. The value shown above is #657888, dropped ~2.7% in lightness to reach 4.57:1 and hold the 4.59:1 the current colour already has. Every other neutral is an exact hue-only retint.

One thing to accept. Retinting costs a little contrast wherever a colour moves away from violet, because blue at 207° carries slightly more luminance than the same colour at 224°. --ink-soft, the body-copy colour, goes from 7.33:1 to 6.63:1 — comfortably AA, but no longer AAA. --ink stays AAA at 14.51:1, and --navy-800 stays far above AA at 12.65:1. Nothing drops below AA.
