About Filipina Cabin Crew

Filipina Cabin Crew is an independent aviation community platform founded in 2016 with a mission to inspire, empower, inform, and connect people across the aviation community.

What began as a platform rooted in real cabin crew experience has grown into a global community for aspiring and current cabin crew of all nationalities, as well as aviation enthusiasts around the world.

For aspiring cabin crew, we share career guidance, global hiring updates, mentorship, and aviation insights to help them better understand the industry and prepare for their journey.

For current cabin crew, we aim to inspire, empower, celebrate, and connect those already working in the industry by sharing stories, experiences, opportunities, industry insights, and meaningful conversations from across the cabin crew community.

For aviation enthusiasts, we share stories, insights, and content that celebrate aviation, the people behind it, and the experiences that make the industry unique.

Filipina Cabin Crew is built on the belief that aviation connects people beyond nationality, airline, or background. Our goal is to create a welcoming global space where people can learn, connect, share experiences, discover opportunities, and celebrate a shared passion for aviation.

Independent & Unaffiliated

Filipina Cabin Crew is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any airline, aviation authority, manufacturer, or other organization featured in its content. Filipina Cabin Crew does not recruit on behalf of any airline and does not charge fees for access to any content or listings on this platform.
