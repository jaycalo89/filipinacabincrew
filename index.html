Est. 2016 • Worldwide Community

Where Aviation Connects Us

Founded by a former Filipina cabin crew member based in the Middle East, now based in the USA, Filipina Cabin Crew is a global aviation community created to inspire, empower, guide, and connect aspiring and current cabin crew of all nationalities worldwide.

Through career guidance, global hiring updates, mentorship, aviation insights, and stories from across the industry, our mission is to create a space where the cabin crew community can learn, grow, connect, and inspire one another.

A global community shaped by real cabin crew experience.
