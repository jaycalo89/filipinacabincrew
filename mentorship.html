Mentorship Program

Personal guidance from experienced aviation professionals — for the people just starting out, and for the crew ready for the next step.

How It Works

Three Steps, No Mystery

Mentorship here is a conversation, not a course. Here is exactly what happens after you apply.

1

Apply

Tell us where you are right now and what you are aiming for. The form takes a few minutes — the more honest the answers, the more useful the first session.

2

Get Matched

We read every application and match you with a mentor whose experience fits your goal, your region and the stage you are at. You will hear back by email either way.

3

Start the Sessions

One-to-one sessions online, scheduled around your roster or your day job, with practical work between them: your CV, your answers, your application plan.

What You Get

Guidance That Fits Your Actual Situation

Generic advice is everywhere and it is free. What is hard to find is someone who will look at your application, in your circumstances, and tell you honestly what is working and what is not.

  • One-on-one guidance tailored to your goals, not a template
  • A line-by-line review of your CV and application answers
  • Mock interview practice, including the questions people freeze on
  • Real insight from inside the aviation industry
  • A realistic plan for which airlines to target, and when
  • Support throughout your application journey — including after a rejection

Questions

Frequently Asked

Who is the mentorship program for?

Anyone working toward, or already working in, a cabin crew career — of any nationality, from anywhere in the world. Most applicants are preparing for their first assessment day, but experienced crew looking at promotion or a move to another airline are equally welcome.

Do I need experience in aviation to apply?

No. A large share of successful cabin crew come from hospitality, retail, healthcare, teaching and customer service. Part of the work we do together is translating the experience you already have into the language airlines recruit on.

How much time does it take?

Plan for a session each week over roughly four weeks, plus the work in between — rewriting your CV, preparing answers, practising. Sessions are scheduled around rosters and time zones.

Can you get me a job with an airline?

No, and you should be cautious of anyone who says they can. Filipina Cabin Crew is an independent community platform — not a recruiter, an agency, or a representative of any airline. We have no influence over any airline's hiring decision. What mentorship does is make sure your application, your preparation and your expectations are as strong as they can be.

Is there a fee?

Any fee, or the absence of one, is stated clearly before you commit to anything — there are no charges hidden inside the process, and we never charge for job placement. If a website, agent or individual asks you to pay for a guaranteed airline job, treat it as a warning sign and check directly with the airline.

What happens to the information I send?

It is used to review and respond to your application, and nothing else. We do not sell or share personal details. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail, including how to ask for your data to be deleted.

What if I am not accepted?

Places are limited, so it happens. Every guide on this site stays free and open, the hiring updates keep coming, and you are welcome to apply again at the next intake.
